# VueJobs India - VueVerse 💚

**VueJobs India - VueVerse** is a community-driven platform by **VueVerse**, dedicated to helping Vue.js developers discover career opportunities, connect with recruiters, and grow within the Vue ecosystem.

Our goal is to build **India's largest Vue.js career platform**, making it easier for developers to find jobs, improve their skills, and thrive in their frontend journey.

---

## Why VueJobs India - VueVerse?

The Vue.js ecosystem is growing rapidly, but Vue-specific opportunities are often scattered across multiple platforms.

VueJobs India - VueVerse aims to bring everything together in one place.

* 💚 Dedicated to Vue.js and Nuxt developers
* 🇮🇳 Focused on the Indian Vue ecosystem
* 🔍 Discover jobs from multiple trusted sources
* 🤝 Community-driven and open source
* 🚀 Built by developers, for developers

---

## Features

### Current Goals

* Search Vue.js jobs
* Filter jobs by location, experience, skills, and more
* Save and track applications
* View job details and company information
* Recruiters can directly post jobs
